Brisbane rental stress: rent as % of household income (2020–2025)

Households spending >30% of income on rent are considered to be in housing stress

Brisbane renters now spend 29% of household income on rent — up from 22% in 2020. At 30% the household crosses into official housing stress, pushing many to seek more affordable options in areas like Canungra.
