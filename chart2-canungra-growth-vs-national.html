Canungra annual house price growth vs national average (2020–2025)

Counter-urbanisation from Brisbane and the Gold Coast has driven Canungra's property price growth to more than five times the national average — a direct economic consequence of urban migrants arriving with city equity.

In 2021 — the peak year of remote-work-enabled counter-urbanisation — Canungra's house prices rose by 33.3%, outpacing even the national COVID-era surge of 24.5%. By 2025, while the national average has settled to 4.3%, Canungra remains at 23.4%: five times the national rate, driven by continued urban migration inflating prices beyond local income levels.
