Canungra annual house price growth vs national average (2020–2025)

Counter-urbanisation has driven Canungra's property price growth to more than five times the national average — direct evidence that urban migration, not local demand, is inflating prices.

In 2021 — the peak year of remote-work-enabled counter-urbanisation — Canungra's house prices rose by 33.3%. By 2025 the national market has settled to 4.3% while Canungra remains at 23.4%: more than five times the national rate.
