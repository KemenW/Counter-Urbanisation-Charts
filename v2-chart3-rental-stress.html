Brisbane rental stress: rent as % of household income (2020–2025)

Households spending more than 30% of income on rent are in official housing stress — the key economic push factor driving counter-urbanisation to Canungra.

Brisbane renters now spend 29% of household income on rent — up from 22% in 2020, approaching the 30% housing stress threshold that pushes households to seek more affordable options in areas like Canungra.
