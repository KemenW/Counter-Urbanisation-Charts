High rents combined with critically low vacancy rates across Brisbane and the Gold Coast create a rental crisis that pushes households toward the Scenic Rim hinterland — where vacancy is equally tight, confirming counter-urbanisation pressure has followed them there.

All three locations sit at critically low vacancy rates well below the 2.6–3.5% considered healthy by the REIQ.
